# UPSC Prelims detailed syllabus - working edition

Editorial expansion, not verbatim official syllabus. The working structure is active locally, including empty topics. Reviewed placements cover all nine subjects; full corpus classification and chapter-specific editorial review are not complete.

Empty concepts remain visible with disabled test actions. Existing unreviewed legacy placements remain distinct from reviewed V2 decisions during migration.

## History

### Ancient India

- **1. Sources and Chronology:** Archaeology and dating; Literary sources; Foreign accounts; Inscriptions and coins; Historical geography.
- **2. Prehistoric Cultures:** Palaeolithic cultures; Mesolithic settlements; Neolithic farming; Chalcolithic cultures; Megalithic traditions.
- **3. Indus Valley Civilisation:** Chronology and sites; Town planning; Agriculture and trade; Crafts and technology; Religion and society; Decline and continuities.
- **4. Vedic Society:** Early Vedic polity; Later Vedic state formation; Social organisation; Economy; Vedic texts and rituals.
- **5. Mahajanapadas:** Sixteen mahajanapadas; Republican states; Rise of Magadha; Urbanisation and coinage.
- **6. Buddhism:** Life and teachings; Councils and patronage; Schools and sects; Texts and languages; Monastic institutions; Spread beyond India.
- **7. Jainism and Philosophical Traditions:** Tirthankaras and teachings; Councils and texts; Sects and patronage; Ajivikas; Schools of Indian philosophy.
- **8. Mauryan Empire:** Sources and chronology; Administration; Ashoka and dhamma; Economy and society; Inscriptions; Art and patronage; Decline.
- **9. Post-Mauryan States:** Indo-Greeks; Shakas and Parthians; Kushanas; Satavahanas; Silk routes and maritime trade; Shungas and Kanvas.
- **10. Sangam Age:** Texts and chronology; Chiefdoms and kingdoms; Society and economy; Ports and external contacts.
- **11. Gupta and Post-Gupta India:** Gupta rulers and administration; Land grants and economy; Learning and science; Harsha; Regional powers.
- **12. Early Medieval Transitions:** Agrarian expansion; Trade guilds; Landholding and polity; Regional languages; Social change.
- **13. Kingship and State Formation:** Royal status and legitimacy.
### Medieval India

- **1. Early Medieval Kingdoms:** Palas and Senas; Gurjara-Pratiharas; Rashtrakutas; Chalukyas; Pallavas; Cholas and local administration; Historical geography.
- **2. Delhi Sultanate:** Slave dynasty; Khaljis; Tughlaqs; Sayyids and Lodis; Administration and iqta; Markets and revenue.
- **3. Regional Kingdoms:** Vijayanagara; Bahmani kingdom; Deccan sultanates; Bengal and Gujarat; Kashmir; Rajput states; Assam and Ahoms.
- **4. Mughal Empire:** Babur and Humayun; Akbar; Jahangir and Shah Jahan; Aurangzeb; Mansabdari and jagirdari; Revenue administration; Succession and decline.
- **5. Marathas and Regional Powers:** Shivaji; Peshwas and confederacy; Sikh powers; Mysore; Awadh and Hyderabad.
- **6. Medieval Society and Economy:** Agrarian relations; Craft production; Trade and towns; Technology; Women and social structures; Village administration.
- **7. Bhakti and Sufi Traditions:** Alvars and Nayanars; Saguna and nirguna traditions; Saints and compositions; Sufi orders; Institutions and practices; Siddhas and Lingayats.
- **8. Sources and Chronology:** Foreign accounts; Literary sources.
### Modern India & National Movement

- **1. European Companies:** Portuguese; Dutch and Danish; English and French; Factories and trading networks; Carnatic conflicts.
- **2. British Expansion:** Bengal conquest; Anglo-Mysore wars; Anglo-Maratha wars; Anglo-Burmese wars; Subsidiary alliance; Annexation policies; Punjab conquest.
- **3. Colonial Administration:** Company government; Crown rule and proclamations; Civil services; Census and surveys; Army and police; Judiciary; Education policies; Press policies; Local self-government.
- **4. Colonial Economy:** Land revenue settlements; Commercialisation of agriculture; Deindustrialisation; Drain of wealth; Economic critics of colonialism; Transport and communications; Famine policies; Modern industries.
- **5. Social and Religious Reform:** Brahmo and Prarthana Samaj; Arya Samaj; Aligarh movement; Ramakrishna movement; Theosophical movement; Anti-caste movements; Women and reform.
- **6. Popular Resistance:** Tribal uprisings; Peasant movements; Civil rebellions; Revolt of 1857; Resistance in princely states.
- **7. Early Nationalism:** Political associations; Congress foundation; Moderates; Extremists; Partition of Bengal; Swadeshi movement.
- **8. Revolutionary and Overseas Movements:** Revolutionary organisations; Ghadar; Home Rule; Indian diaspora; INA and Subhas Bose.
- **9. Gandhian Movements:** Early satyagrahas; Rowlatt Satyagraha; Non-Cooperation and Khilafat; Congress sessions and resolutions; Civil Disobedience; Round Table Conferences; Quit India; Constructive programmes.
- **10. Constitutional Developments:** Acts before 1858; Acts of 1858 to 1909; Act of 1919; Simon Commission and Nehru Report; Act of 1935; Provincial autonomy and ministries; Cripps and Cabinet Mission; Independence Act; Chronology of constitutional politics.
- **11. Participation in Nationalism:** Workers and trade unions; Peasants; Women; Dalit movements; Socialist and communist currents.
- **12. Independence and Integration:** Partition; Transfer of power; Princely state integration; Constituent Assembly context; Reorganisation background; Bhoodan and land reform movements.
### Art & Culture

- **1. Architecture:** Architectural elements; Stupas and monasteries; Rock-cut architecture; Nagara temples; Dravida temples; Vesara and regional temples; Indo-Islamic architecture; Colonial architecture.
- **2. Sculpture and Iconography:** Harappan objects; Mauryan sculpture; Gandhara and Mathura; Gupta sculpture; Bronze traditions; Buddhist Jain and Hindu iconography.
- **3. Painting:** Prehistoric painting; Murals; Mughal miniatures; Rajput and Pahari schools; Deccan painting; Folk and tribal painting; Modern artists.
- **4. Music:** Hindustani traditions; Carnatic traditions; Forms and composers; Musical instruments; Folk music.
- **5. Dance and Performance:** Classical dances; Folk and tribal dances; Theatre; Puppetry; Martial performance traditions; Postures and gestures.
- **6. Literature and Languages:** Classical languages; Sanskrit literature; Pali and Prakrit; Tamil literature; Persian and Urdu; Regional literature; Scripts and inscriptions.
- **7. Living Heritage:** Festivals and rituals; Symbols and regalia; Calendars and eras; Crafts and textiles; Oral traditions; Religious and philosophical traditions; Cultural landscapes; Traditional units and measures.
- **8. Heritage Preservation:** Archaeological institutions; Museums and archives; World Heritage criteria; Intangible heritage; Conservation legislation.

- **7. The Atmosphere, Insolation, Heat Budget and Temperature:** Composition; Structure; Ionosphere; Solar Radiation and Insolation; Scattering, Reflection and Albedo; Heat Budget of the Earth; How the Atmosphere Is Heated and Cooled; Greenhouse Effect and Global Warming; Ozone Layer; Lapse Rate, Adiabatic Processes and Inversion; Temperature Distribution; Weather, Climate and Humidity; Weather Instruments; Air Pollution.
