# Paper-processing instructions

For any PYQ paper intake, reconstruction, classification, answer verification,
correction or publication task, read `docs/paper-processing-sop.md` completely
before acting. Follow its linked reproduction, exception and release guidance
for the work being performed. Use the review templates; do not treat blank
templates or previous paper approvals as evidence for a new paper.

Process one selected complete paper at a time. Do not start another paper or
silently omit English, Science, Mathematics, CSAT or any numbered section.
Keep source exam identity separate from preparation-target applicability.

Use authentic matching official answers, clearly distinguishing final and
provisional keys. When no official key is recovered, only documented primary
evidence plus a distinct second pass may establish a reasoned answer. A faithful
officially keyed PYQ remains scored against that key with any editorial caveat
separate. Never substitute an unscored viewer for a completed structured import.

Preserve raw imports, canonical IDs, submitted choices and historical snapshots.
Apply reviewed overlays with before-hash guards and independent answer/content/
taxonomy revisions. Do not invent source wording, evidence or reviewer approval.

The user's standing paper-release instruction is: complete, self-review, test,
push through the existing GitHub workflow and verify production without asking
for routine question-by-question approval. A later user instruction to work
locally, stop, or withhold release takes precedence. This does not authorise
unrelated papers, database migrations, infrastructure changes, force pushes or
destructive rollback. Do not call a release live until its live checks pass.
